Atelier Client Questions

Clear information regarding orders, deliveries, fittings, and exchanges.

We provide complimentary express delivery on all orders above ₹5,000. Orders within metropolitan regions (Bangalore, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata) typically arrive within 2 to 3 business days via insured express couriers. Orders under ₹5,000 carry a nominal ₹250 delivery fee.

Handloom silks should always be stored in breathable pure unbleached cotton muslin (which we provide with every purchase). Avoid direct plastic covers. We recommend gentle professional dry cleaning and changing the saree fold lines every four months to preserve the delicate metallic zari integrity.

We offer a 14-day hassle-free doorstep exchange and return policy on unworn, unaltered items with original boutique tags intact. Our courier will pick up the package directly from your home, and refunds are processed within 48 hours of inspection.
